Admission Process

Complete the following steps to successfully submit your application for admission.

1

New User and Registration

Create a new account by providing your basic personal and contact information.

Start Registration
2

Verify via SMS and E-mail

Verify your registered mobile number and email address using the verification code sent to you.

3

Sign in for Registered User

Login using your Application ID and password created during registration.

Login Now
4

Personal and Education Details

Complete your personal, address, educational and course-related information.

5

Payment of Application Fees

Pay the application processing fee of ₹500 using the available online payment options.

6

Photo & Signature Upload

Upload or capture your photograph and upload your signature according to the specified requirements.

7

Upload Required Documents

Upload the required educational, identity and supporting documents.

8

Download Filled Application

Review your completed application and download the final application form for your records.
